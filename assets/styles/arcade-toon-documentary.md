# Arcade Toon Documentary

Use for authored scenes in which 2D toons inhabit ordinary human spaces. This
is the house visual language: drawn enough to remain magical, integrated enough
to feel physically present.

Build every image around one reportable moment. Give a single protagonist a
specific behavior, put one secondary reaction in conversation with it, and make
every supporting figure direct the eye back toward that event. Leave deliberate
quiet space. Treat the setting as evidence, not set dressing.

# Toon in Real World

Use when the desired effect is an impossible stylized character placed inside an
ordinary, unchanged real photo. The visible contrast between real background
and cartoon subject is the point. The character must still physically belong
there: give them a contact shadow, the room's reflected color, correct
perspective, and any foreground occlusion the photo requires.

For a generated group scene, use the **Arcade scene brief** before prompting:

1. Name one protagonist and the exact moment that has just changed.
2. State the protagonist's visible want, the secondary reaction, and where the
   eye lands first.
3. Arrange everyone else to point, lean, look, or gesture toward that beat.
4. Reserve quiet space and separate foreground, middle ground, and background.

The result should feel reported from a lived-in Toontown, not decorated with
interchangeable cute figures.

# Moxie

## Construction lock

Moxie's face reads first through the purple cat-eye glasses, golden eyes,
compact fox muzzle, and swept auburn/mauve hair. Keep the lines readable and
her head-to-body relationship deliberately toon-like, not human-photoreal.

Her dark hand and forearm socks are **natural fur markings**, not gloves. They
begin over the digits/paws and end naturally midway between wrist and elbow.
When hands are interacting with a glass, controller, chair, phone, or utensil,
the grip and gesture win over showing every claw.

## Character acting

Prefer behavior over label-based emotion:

- one ear tracking trouble while her face stays composed;
- a tail unconsciously securing itself around a chair leg;
- holding a reaction for half a beat before the laugh arrives;
- an over-careful grip on a drink or controller when pretending to be relaxed;
- golden eyes looking through the cat-eye frames at the actual point of action.

Characteristic expressions: a small knowing smile; one brow lifting behind the
cat-eye frame; lips pressed while deciding whether to say it; bright-eyed
delight arriving a beat late; and a deliberately neutral face betrayed by one
attentive ear. Keep each readable at a glance.

## Photo transformation boundary

For an existing photo, change only the identified person. Preserve crop,
framing, pose, clothing silhouette and graphics, objects, setting, and all
other people. The toon/photo mismatch is intentional; physical conviction
comes from correct contact, occlusion, perspective, contact shadow, and the
room's reflected color—not from turning Moxie photoreal.
